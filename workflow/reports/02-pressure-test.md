# Pressure test

## Result

The Original concentration claim survives. China is 57.2% of the supplied World EV fleet row and 57.9% of the sum of the 59 country/economy rows. The five largest country fleets are 77.7% and 78.7% under those two denominators. The result is not created by the regional rows or asynchronous dates among the top five, which are all dated September 2026.

The energy-impact interpretation needs stronger qualification. Reported oil displacement per EV ranges from 2.277 to 9.561 boe/year across countries, a more than fourfold spread, while the files do not document the impact method.

## Strongest supported conclusion

The supplied data shows that the current EV fleet is highly concentrated: China alone represents about 57–58% and the five largest country fleets about 78–79%, depending on denominator. The fleet’s reported energy impact is also concentrated, but cross-country impact comparisons should not be interpreted without the missing methodology.

## Unsupported stronger claims

- The dataset cannot establish that a particular policy caused the fleet concentration.
- It cannot independently verify actual oil consumption avoided.
- It cannot show a fully synchronized October 2026 global snapshot.
- It cannot explain why reported per-vehicle displacement differs across countries.
