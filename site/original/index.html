57.2% of the world’s EV fleet is in one market.

The dataset reports a global fleet of 90.6 million vehicles. China accounts for more than half of that stock—and a similar share of the oil displacement associated with it.

What this means

EV adoption is spreading, but today’s energy impact remains highly concentrated.

What it does not mean

These files do not establish which policies caused adoption or document the impact model.

A large fleet does not guarantee fast current adoption.

China combines both: 51.8 million EVs and a 67% share of new registrations in September 2026. The United States has the second-largest fleet, but its latest monthly share is 8.2%. Norway is the reverse: a smaller stock and an almost entirely electric new-car market.

The next wave is moving at different speeds.

Choose a market to see the monthly share of registrations that were battery-electric or plug-in hybrid. Monthly values can be volatile, so this is direction—not a forecast.
