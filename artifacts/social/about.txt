About this project
The EV transition series is an independent data-visualisation project built entirely from public data: Ember's Monthly EV Sales Data (CC BY 4.0), with monthly EV registrations by market (2005 to October 2026) and a latest-available EV fleet and oil-displaced snapshot. Every number can be reproduced from the published source.

How it was made
I built it with my own approval-gated AI framework. A public dataset goes in; four reviewed artifacts come out, and a person approves each stage before the next one starts. The intro video, infographic and these posts come out of the same workflow.

① Public dataset in: monthly EV sales + fleet impact
   ↓ profile + context
② Original analysis: strongest defensible story
   ↓ human review + approval
③ Pressure-tested deep-dive: checks claims, limits
   ↓ human review + approval
④ Dashboard: validated measures only
   ↓ human review + approval
⑤ Data story web app: poppy design language, live on GitHub
   ↓ final approval
⑥ /artifact + /post-social: video + infographic, every format
